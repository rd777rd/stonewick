Our Story

Stonewick started with a simple frustration: every candle jar we loved ended up in the trash the moment the wax ran out. We wanted the ritual of lighting a candle without the waste — so we built a system where you buy a beautiful stone or ceramic vessel once, and refill it for years with compostable pouches.

Sustainability, in practice

Our refill pouches are compostable, our vessels are designed to last a lifetime, and our scent rotation is built around what actually matters to you — the memory a scent brings back, not a marketing term like "top notes."
